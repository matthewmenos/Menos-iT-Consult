Our Mission

Putting technology to work for small businesses

At Menos iT Consult, our mission is to provide top-notch IT consulting services to small businesses. We strive to help our clients optimize their technology solutions to improve efficiency and productivity.

Based in Agona, Western Ghana, we understand the unique challenges facing businesses in our region. We combine global best practices with local insight to deliver solutions that truly work for you — not just on paper, but in practice.

Our Journey

From a small office to a trusted partner

2023

Founded in Agona

Menos iT Consult opens its doors with a team of three, focused on network support for local businesses.

2024

Cybersecurity Practice Launched

Growing demand for digital security leads us to build a dedicated cybersecurity team and gain our first enterprise client.

2024

Cloud Services Added

We help our first client migrate to AWS, cutting their infrastructure costs by 35%. Cloud becomes a cornerstone offering.

2025

100+ Clients Milestone

We celebrate 100 businesses served across Ghana and begin expanding our team to meet growing regional demand.

2026

Today — Still Growing

150+ clients, a full-service IT consultancy, and the same commitment to honest, practical technology solutions we started with.
