This Cookie Policy explains how Menos iT Consult uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. We keep cookie use to a minimum — we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, remember your preferences, and provide anonymised information to site owners about how visitors use the site.

Cookies set by the website you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by third-party services (such as embedded maps or fonts) are called third-party cookies.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies — temporary files that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — files that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

04 Managing Cookies

You have full control over cookies through your browser settings. You can choose to block or delete all cookies, or configure your browser to alert you before accepting a cookie. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent parts of the website from working correctly.

How to manage cookies in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit allaboutcookies.org.
